## 3. Key Areas of Defensive Security

**Security Operations Centre (SOC):**
A team responsible for monitoring an organisation's systems, identifying threats and responding to security incidents.

**Threat Intelligence:**
Collecting and analysing information about potential cyber threats to help organisations improve their security.

**Digital Forensics:**
Investigating digital evidence to understand security incidents and determine what happened.

**Incident Response:**
The process of identifying, investigating, containing and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

## 4. Key Takeaways

- Continuous monitoring helps organisations identify suspicious activities.
- SOC analysts investigate alerts and potential security incidents.
- Threat intelligence helps organisations understand attacker behaviour.
- Incident response helps minimise the impact of cyberattacks.
