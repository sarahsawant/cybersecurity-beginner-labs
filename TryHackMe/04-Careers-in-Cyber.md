## 3. Cybersecurity Career Paths

**Offensive Security:**
Focuses on identifying vulnerabilities and testing the security of systems through authorised security assessments.

**Defensive Security:**
Focuses on protecting systems, monitoring suspicious activities and responding to security threats.

**SOC Analyst:**
Monitors security alerts, investigates suspicious events and supports incident response.

## 4. Key Takeaways

- Cybersecurity offers multiple career opportunities.
- Technical knowledge and problem-solving are important skills.
- Continuous learning is essential because cyber threats constantly evolve.
- Practical experience helps build professional cybersecurity skills.
